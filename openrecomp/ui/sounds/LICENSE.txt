OpenRecomp original UI sounds

All menu sounds in this folder and the runtime theme cues synthesized by
openrecomp/ui_sound.py are original OpenRecomp work, covered by the repository's
MIT LICENSE. No audio samples, recordings, compositions or sound effects from
Namco, Bandai Namco, a game installation or a third-party sound pack are used.

The existing WAV files are reproducible with tools/make_ui_sounds.py. T4's light
bell cues and T5DR's sharper impact cues are computed from sine waves and FM on
the audio worker, then cached only in memory. No audio device is polled while
idle, and there is no background music or looping audio.
